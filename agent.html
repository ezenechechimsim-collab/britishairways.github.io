Biography

Captain Richard Azuama joined British Airways as a First Officer in 2008 after training at the British Airways Flying College, and was promoted to Captain in 2015. Over an eighteen-year career, he has flown the Boeing 777 and 787 fleets on long-haul routes connecting London Heathrow to Lagos, Cairo, Dubai, and New York — accumulating more than 14,200 flight hours.

Before joining British Airways, Richard earned his commercial pilot's licence and instrument rating in the United Kingdom, having grown up between Lagos and London. Colleagues describe him as calm under pressure and meticulous in the cockpit, with a particular focus on mentoring newly qualified First Officers during their first years of long-haul flying.

Outside the flight deck, Richard is an advocate for aviation careers in West Africa, regularly speaking to students in Lagos about routes into commercial flying. He holds type ratings on the Boeing 777 and 787, and maintains current certification in crew resource management and emergency procedures.

"Every flight is someone's first trip abroad, or their trip home to family. That responsibility never gets old — it's why I still love this job as much as I did on day one."
