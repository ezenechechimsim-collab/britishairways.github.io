Every detail, taken care of.

From the moment you book to the moment you land, British Airways is built around one idea: travel should feel effortless. Here's everything included in your journey.

What we offer

Core services available across our network, on every cabin from Economy to First.

💺

Ergonomic Seating

Adjustable headrests, a pillow, and a blanket on every seat — with lie-flat beds and extra recline in our premium cabins.

🍽️

Food & Drink

A complimentary three-course meal, snacks, and full bar service — hot, cold, and alcoholic drinks — on all long-haul flights.

🎬

Entertainment

A personal screen at every seat loaded with the latest movies, boxsets, music, and games, plus live flight-path tracking.

🧳

Baggage

Generous checked and cabin baggage allowances, priority handling for premium fares, and real-time bag tracking in the app.

🛋️

Airport Lounges

Relax before departure in our Galleries and Concorde Room lounges — shower suites, dining, and quiet workspaces included.

🧑‍🦽

Special Assistance

Dedicated support for reduced mobility, unaccompanied minors, and travellers with additional needs, arranged ahead of your flight.

🌐

Wi-Fi & Connectivity

Stay connected at altitude with on-board Wi-Fi across the fleet, so you can message, browse, or work from 35,000 feet.

🏅

Executive Club

Earn Avios on every flight and unlock tier points toward Bronze, Silver, and Gold status with priority boarding and upgrades.
